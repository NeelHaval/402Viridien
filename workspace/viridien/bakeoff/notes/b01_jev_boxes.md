# B01: JEV boxes and legend patterns

`locate(log)` uses a bounded header/body crop and never reads `log.boxes`. It requests three JEV boxes when the service is reachable, snaps proposed column edges to nearby long vertical lines, and otherwise uses a rudimentary line-and-position fallback. The module entry point scores predictions against annotations and writes the comparison to `out/b01`. A filled outline is not used: true boxes are thicker, predictions thinner, with depth blue, lithology red and legend green. On extremely tall pages the full-page overview necessarily makes small horizontal errors hard to see.

The interval baseline compares colour histograms and directional ink differences to parsed legend swatches, with long dark rows as possible boundaries. It removes persistent dark border columns from feature calculations. Its three pipeline panels show source, rules and matched mean swatch colours.

Limitations: the JEV endpoint refused connections in this environment, so JEV predictions and the requested with-JEV IoUs could not be measured. Where `jev_used` is false, the prediction IoU columns describe the fallback, not a JEV experiment. The fallback does not yet perform OCR-based increasing-depth verification, swatch-density legend refinement, or full-page content extent detection; its results should not be used as reliable box locations. The interval classifier is also experimental: its swatch histogram and texture features do not reliably distinguish visually similar patterns, and blockwise decisions create spurious boundaries. Legend parsing and depth calibration are shared harness dependencies.
