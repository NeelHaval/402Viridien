YOUR STRATEGY: id=s03_template_match, file strategies/s03_template_match.py
S3 template matching (CPU only). For each is_lithology swatch: strip its border, tile it, scale so its pattern period matches the column's (try a few scales, keep the best correlation). Slide over the column in tiles about one swatch tall, score every swatch by normalised cross-correlation (grey, or per channel for colour) plus a colour-histogram term, take the best per tile, merge equal neighbours, refine each boundary to the row where the scores cross. Cluster id = assigned swatch.
