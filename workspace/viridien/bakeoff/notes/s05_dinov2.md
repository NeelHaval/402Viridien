# S5 DINOv2

The model is loaded once per process from the local Hugging Face cache. Overlapping 96-pixel tiles are repeated to square, resized to 224, embedded on the selected device, and segmented with PELT. Full-width dark rules refine candidate contacts; agglomerative cosine clustering groups segment means. Cluster names minimize cosine distance plus 0.30 times normalized Lab distance to lithology swatches. White runs are omitted. Four stage images show the input, embeddings, candidate contacts, and legend colours.

The completed synthetic run before the final edge-only blank-detection adjustment scored 0.757 mean (row accuracy 0.946, boundary F1 0.796, ARI 0.814) across 39 windows, with zero crashes. The weight was selected as a heuristic and was not subjected to a systematic sweep. Synthetic images show successful separation of strongly coloured swatches and a tendency to confuse similar monochrome patterns. Repeated horizontal pattern strokes initially caused false boundaries; merging adjacent same-label predictions improved the synthetic boundary F1. A real log06 image showed blank rows being labelled due to the dark border, motivating the final edge exclusion.

The gold log06 rendering shows additional failures: shale is matched to silty sandstone, and the legend's exact 'Sand and Sandstone' label differs from gold's 'Sandstone'. Gold score therefore cannot be interpreted as texture detection alone. Candidate improvements include scale/phase-robust swatch embeddings, finer texture descriptors and colour weighting tuned across synthetic logs, more reliable blank detection, and a shared label-normalization policy if the scoring contract permits it.
