Keyboard help

Drag inside the lithology column to draw; click a box to select. Drag its edges to resize. Hold the pointer near the top or bottom of the image while dragging to scroll automatically. Shift-click selects several. Each bed has one rock at 100%. Space+drag or Pan mode moves the page.

1–9,0 assign a rock. Tab cycles groups of ten. / or typing opens the picker; arrows and Enter select. Esc closes it, leaving an unassigned box. F draws full width from the last bottom to the cursor. U toggles uncertain for a layer. Del deletes selected boxes. J/K move between layers. +/− zoom; Ctrl+wheel zooms at the cursor. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo, Shift+Z redo.
